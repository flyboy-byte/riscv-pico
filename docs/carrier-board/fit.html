Drag parts around the board, drawn to scale (one hole is 2.54 mm). Lines are the connections from the repo pinout. A red circle is a crossing, which on one layer means a jumper wire.

Parts

Sizes marked est. are typical for these kinds of modules, so measure yours and type them in. Only the Pico's pin positions are exact. Pins on the SD, OLED, VGA, converter and PSRAM boards follow typical layouts. The ElectroCookie size comes from a retailer listing. The PS/2 screw-terminal breakout from the cart had no size listed. Power and clock notes under each part come from the riscv-pico docs, and they matter for placement as much as size does.
